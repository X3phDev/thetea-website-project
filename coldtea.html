Cold Tea: Things to consider before adopting a dog

By The Tea Staff | 01/27/2026

Adopting a dog may seem like a good idea however there are some things you must consider before deciding on adopting a dog. For starters, a dog is a huge financial commitment as they need regular food and water, bedding, toys for enrichment, and consistent exercise. These all require some form of income so the dog you choose can be happy.

To provide a dog with a happy life you first need to make sure they are being fed on a regular schedule with a regular diet. You also need to consistently play with them, especially if they are a puppy. Walking your dog is another activity you need to consider as walking a dog requires a leash as well as some bags to pick up after your dog. After all, nobody likes a messy street.

Finally, your dog will reward you by showering you with affection, loyalty, and many other personality traits. Dogs are man's best friend for a reason and they tend to stick by your side regardless of what happens.
