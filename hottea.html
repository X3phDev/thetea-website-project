Hot Tea: Free Adoption Event in South Carolina

By The Tea Staff | 01/27/2026

Adoption events are often tricky and costly and usually end up in either a lot of paperwork, money spent, or even missing out on a potential companion. Fee free adoption events aim to curb these issues and offer a 100% fee free adoption to empty shelters and provide dogs homes. According to PickMeSC exactly 2416 pets were adopted last year alone which is a staggering number. I was lucky enough to adopt one of my own pets from such an event and I think it's best if I talk about the experience.

I first became aware of PickMeSC when I was researching possible cheap dog adoptions and it just so happened that the event was happening that exact same week. I managed to find a few locations and set up a few meetings with some dogs. They were all great and I would have loved to rescue them all but I found the one dog who resonated with me. After picking my dog I was given a free bag of dog food, a collar, a lead, and some paperwork to declare that the dog was officially mine. Overall, the staff were pleasant and the dog I now have with me has been one of the best decisions of my life.

So if you have the chance please consider adopting a dog or a cat or any animal from a shelter as their lives could be saved and they could change your life in ways you couldn't even imagine how
